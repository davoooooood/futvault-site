6 Escrow Arbitration & Symmetric Buyer Dispute Bond

Disputes are evaluated using live Market Observer auction telemetry, cryptographic Re-List Challenges, coin-delta audit snapshots, and challenge-coded FV-XXXX screen-recording verification.

To prevent false refund framing, opening a contested buyer dispute carries a symmetric refundable $1.50 USDT Buyer Dispute Bond. This dispute bond is automatically waived on a clean buyer's first dispute against an unbonded, under-collateralized, or unverified seller (and whenever Market Observers verify an active or expired-unsold listing). Valid disputes return 100% of the dispute bond alongside the refund; repeated false disputes forfeit the bond and permanently blacklist the offender.
