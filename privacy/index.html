1 Strict Zero-KYC (No-KYC) Policy

FUTVault is built on sovereign identity minimization. We never collect, request, verify, or store government-issued IDs, national ID cards, passports, driver's licenses, biometric selfies, proof-of-address utility bills, or custodial fiat KYC dossiers from buyers, coin sellers, or B2B partners.

2 Pseudonymous Operational Data Processed

To match P2P orders, verify on-chain settlement, and arbitrate escrow disputes, FUTVault processes strictly pseudonymous operational records:

  • Pseudonymous Account Identifiers: Telegram numeric User ID (tg:<id>), optional public @username, and chosen UI language (en, fa, ar), cryptographically authenticated via Telegram WebApp initData (HMAC-SHA256) or direct Telegram Bot updates.
  • Non-Custodial Crypto & Ledger Records: Deterministic watch-only deposit invoice addresses (TON Connect / USDT Jetton + Memo, USDT TRC20, USDT BEP20), public blockchain transaction hashes (txHash), user-provided payout destination addresses, and security/dispute bond ledger balances. Private wallet seed phrases or cold-wallet keys are never requested or stored.
  • Escrow & Anti-Fraud Records: Order/tranche state, Transfer Market card metadata (player card IDs, watermark prices, blind card attributes), scoped session access tokens (fvb_*, fvs_*, fvspt_*), and user-submitted dispute verification evidence (FV-XXXX screen recordings or screenshots with perceptual anti-replay fingerprints).

3 Zero-Retention Ephemeral Credential Policy

FUTVault's default Passwordless Open Bid flow requires zero EA account passwords or backup codes.

If credentialed Comfort Trade transfer is ever explicitly enabled and chosen for an order, credentials and one-time backup codes are protected under a strict Zero-Retention architecture:

  • Authenticated Encryption: Encrypted in transit over TLS and encrypted in ephemeral memory / crash-recovery storage using AES-256-GCM with per-order associated data (AAD = orderId) and a bounded 26-hour maximum TTL.
  • Immediate Cryptographic Purge: The moment an order transitions to any terminal state (COMPLETED, REFUNDED, or CANCELLED) or upon TTL expiry, all credential buffers are cryptographically zeroed (Buffer.fill(0)) and deleted permanently.
  • Strict Backup Exclusion: Encrypted credential recovery rows (futvault_order_credentials) are explicitly excluded from all PostgreSQL dumps and SQLite snapshot backups.

4 Counterparty Redaction & Public Data Minimization

All public order and ticket views automatically redact counterparty identities (tg:[REDACTED], seller:[REDACTED]), payout wallet destinations, access tokens, and blind card attributes so other marketplace participants cannot scrape personal handles, wallets, or market watermarks.

5 No Third-Party Advertising Trackers or Cookies

FUTVault uses zero third-party advertising networks, behavioral profiling scripts, analytics pixels, or cross-site tracking cookies. These public legal pages make zero third-party network requests for external fonts or trackers. When launching the Mini-App from @FUTVaultExchangeBot, only essential functional browser storage (localStorage / sessionStorage) is used to preserve active order IDs, language selection, and scoped access tokens locally on your device.
